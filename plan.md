PolyRhythm is a rhythm game that switches between rhythm games during the song. For example, you could start by playing Stepmania, then a minute into the song, switch to osu!, then a little bit later it switches to taiko. The goal is to score as high as possible (duh) and your score is based on your accuracy and combo.

Mode
- Stepmania
- osu!
- taiko
- Rhythm Typer
- Clone Hero

As a temporary measure, the game should be able to take in .osu files and use those to make the map for the song. This won't be perfect, as .osu files for osu!standard difficulties only have timing points, screen positions, and hit objects.

At first, each mode will be implemented as a separate program, and then will be merged into one program later.
